Every Sunday at 8:30 pm there is a jam at Collin's Irish Tavern, Calle Sagasta 26. It has been going for about ten years and it is where the association was born: bluegrass, old time and americana.

Come to play, whatever your instrument, or just to listen and have a drink. All the details are on [the jams page](/en/jams/).
